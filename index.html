The place

A rooftop, a sunset,
and a cheese roll worth ordering.

EROS coffee sits above Vijay Millennium at Powai Naka, with turf underfoot, lounge sofas, and open sky. It's a meet-up spot for friends and family.

Guests praise the quick service, the clean seating, and the food.

Know before you go

Locals recommend the veg cheese rolls and the special chicken cheese rolls.
